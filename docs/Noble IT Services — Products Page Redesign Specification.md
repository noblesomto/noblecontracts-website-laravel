# Noble IT Services — Products Page Redesign Specification

**Target URL:** `/products`  
**Website:** `https://nobleitservices.ng/`

## 1. Objective

Redesign and rewrite the `/products` page so it presents Noble IT Services as a **professional software development, SaaS and AI product company**.

The page should showcase products Noble IT Services has built, launched and/or operates, while demonstrating the company's product engineering capabilities.

The final page should feel like the product portfolio of a modern software/product engineering company — **not an internal development inventory, startup roadmap or generic digital agency portfolio.**

---

# 2. Important Implementation Rules

### MUST

- Preserve the existing application's framework, architecture and localization.
- Do not change unrelated pages or functionality.
- Preserve existing routes unless a route change is explicitly required.
- Reuse existing components and styling conventions where appropriate.
- Maintain responsive behaviour across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Maintain accessibility and semantic HTML.
- Maintain existing SEO infrastructure unless improvements are specifically required.
- Preserve working product links and existing functionality.
- Use real product information already available in the project.
- Use real product screenshots/assets where available.
- Do not invent statistics, clients, product metrics or achievements.
- Do not introduce placeholder text into the final page.
- Keep the page fast and optimized.

### DO NOT

- Do not redesign the entire website while working on `/products`.
- Do not modify localization settings.
- Do not remove existing products.
- Do not invent product features.
- Do not expose internal development information unnecessarily.
- Do not make the page overly technical.
- Do not present Noble IT Services primarily as a web-design or digital-marketing agency.
- Do not use generic stock imagery where actual product screenshots are available.

# 26. UX Requirements

The redesigned page must be:

- Fully responsive
- Mobile-first where appropriate
- Keyboard accessible
- Screen-reader friendly
- Semantically structured
- Fast-loading
- Visually consistent with the rest of the website
- Easy to scan
- Easy to navigate

Ensure:

- Proper heading hierarchy
- Accessible buttons and links
- Descriptive image alt text
- Adequate touch-target sizes
- Visible focus states
- Good contrast
- No layout shifts caused by images

---

# 27. Animation Requirements

Use animation sparingly.

Recommended:

- Subtle card reveal
- Gentle image movement
- Hover states
- Smooth category filtering
- Subtle CTA interaction

Avoid:

- Excessive parallax
- Large spinning animations
- Distracting background effects
- Animation that delays content visibility
- Excessive hover effects on mobile

The website should feel **premium and professional**, not flashy.
