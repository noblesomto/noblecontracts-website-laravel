I reviewed the current About Us page. The main issue is that it still reflects the old “web design/digital marketing agency” identity, while the direction we've established for Noble IT Services is now software development, SaaS, AI and digital product engineering.

There are also several obvious quality issues—placeholder statistics, outdated copy, grammatical errors, and duplicated/legacy content—that make the page feel less established than the actual work you're presenting.

1. The positioning needs to change completely

The current opening says:

“We are Professional Digital Team”

and describes Noble IT Services primarily as a company providing websites and digital marketing.

That conflicts with the new homepage positioning:

Software · SaaS · AI

The About page should instead answer:

Who is Noble IT Services?
What kind of company are you?
What do you build?
Who do you build for?
What makes you different?
Why should a business trust you?
How do you approach software development?
Recommended positioning

We Build Software That Moves Businesses Forward.

Noble IT Services is a software development and technology company focused on building custom software, SaaS platforms and AI-powered solutions for businesses, startups and organisations.

2. Replace the current opening section

The current paragraph contains phrases such as “subcidiary,” “registered in Nigerian,” “web designers,” and “outstanding image,” which sound dated and contain grammatical problems.

I'd replace the entire opening with:

About Noble IT Services
We Build Software That Moves Businesses Forward.

Noble IT Services is a software development and technology company focused on building custom software, SaaS platforms and AI-powered solutions.

We work with businesses, entrepreneurs and organisations to transform ideas, business processes and complex requirements into reliable digital products.

From the first product concept to design, development, integration, deployment and ongoing improvement, we provide the engineering expertise required to turn technology into a practical business advantage.

Our experience spans web applications, SaaS platforms, business automation, AI integration, marketplaces, payment systems and other technology-driven solutions.

We don't just build software. We build products designed to solve real problems.

4. Tell the company's story

The current About page doesn't really tell the visitor how Noble IT Services evolved. It jumps straight into generic service descriptions.

I'd add a short company story.

From Web Development to Software Engineering

Noble IT Services has evolved alongside the technology landscape.

What began with web design and digital development has grown into a broader software engineering practice focused on building complete digital products and business platforms.

Today, our work extends beyond websites. We design and develop SaaS products, business applications, marketplaces, AI-powered systems, APIs and integrated digital solutions.

This evolution has been driven by one simple principle:

Technology should solve a business problem, not simply exist as a feature.

That principle guides how we approach every project — from the first conversation to production and beyond.

This is particularly valuable because the existing site still has a lot of legacy digital-agency content, including web design, SEO, SMS marketing and email marketing.
